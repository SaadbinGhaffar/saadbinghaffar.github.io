Blob Jam: Parking Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Blob Jam: Parking Puzzle ("the game") is published by FathomStack ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Game data

Your game progress (level, coins, boosters, unlocked characters and settings) is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Online rankings

To show the weekly, world and country rankings, the game sends the following to our ranking server (hosted by Supabase):

  • a random player ID created on your device (it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account),
  • the player name and character you choose (visible to other players),
  • your country, estimated from your device's time zone and language settings (visible to other players as a two-letter code),
  • the number of levels you have completed and when.

We do not require an account and we do not collect your real name, email or contacts.

Deleting your data

To delete your ranking data, email saadghaffar97@gmail.com with the player name shown in the game. We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device. Advertising data is controlled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Advertising

Blob Jam is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and app diagnostics.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the game you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Without it you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Watching rewarded video ads is always optional.

Children

Blob Jam is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
