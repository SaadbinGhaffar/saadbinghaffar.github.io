Your result first

  • ⛔ Contains: an allergen you chose is in the ingredients or allergen information.
  • ⚠️ May contain: it appears in the "may contain" (traces) information.
  • ❓ Can't check: the product's ingredients or allergens haven't been provided.
  • ✅ Nothing flagged: none of your allergens were found in the listed ingredients.

We never say a product is "safe". If information is missing, the app tells you instead of guessing.

Private by design

No account and no sign-in. Your allergies, diet choices, history and favourites stay on your phone. The camera only reads barcodes; no photos are taken or stored. Label Lens is free and supported by ads. Read the privacy policy.

Important: Label Lens gives information from Open Food Facts, which may be incomplete. It is not medical advice. If you have an allergy, always check the packaging.
