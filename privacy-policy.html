Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

In short: Label Lens has no accounts and no sign-in. Your profile (region, allergies, diet choices), scan history and favourites are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them. To look up a product, the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. The app shows Google AdMob ads, and Google uses some device data for advertising. You can control this in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options (where required) and in your phone's Google settings.

Who we are

Label Lens is an Android app made by FathomStack ("we", "us"). It is available in the United Kingdom, the United States, the European Union, the European Economic Area, Switzerland and Canada. Contact: saadghaffar97@gmail.com.

We do not run any servers that receive information about you. The services named below (Open Food Facts and Google) receive data directly from the app, as described.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile: your region (UK, US, Europe or Canada), the allergens you choose and your diet choices (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal label, low sugar).
  • Your settings: app language, light or dark appearance and whether you finished the welcome screens.
  • Scan history and favourites.
  • A copy of product information (a cache), so history and favourites work offline. Cached products that are in neither your history nor your favourites are removed automatically after about 60 days.

To suggest your region the first time you open the app, Label Lens reads your phone's country setting. This happens on the phone and is not sent anywhere.

We cannot see any of this information. You can delete your scan history in the app (Settings → Clear history), remove favourites one by one, change your profile at any time, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes, and this happens on your phone. No photos or videos are taken, stored or uploaded. The app does not record audio and does not request the microphone.

Open Food Facts (product information)

When you scan or type a barcode, the app sends that barcode to Open Food Facts, a free, community-run food database operated by a non-profit association in France, to get the product's details. Product photos are loaded from Open Food Facts' servers. Like any website, their servers receive your phone's IP address and a technical description of the app (its name and version). We do not send your profile, allergies, diet, history, favourites or any identifier to Open Food Facts.

If you ask for similar products ("Show alternatives"), the app sends the product's category (for example "crumpets") and your country to Open Food Facts' search service (search.openfoodfacts.org), then loads those products like any other. Your allergies, diet and profile are never sent: the check against your profile happens on your phone.

How Open Food Facts handles data is described in the Open Food Facts privacy policy. Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL); images are under CC BY-SA.

Read aloud, sharing and links

  • Read aloud: if you tap the speaker button on a result, the text is spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech service (part of Android). Label Lens does not send it to us or to anyone else.
  • Sharing: only when you tap Share, the product name (or a comparison) and its Open Food Facts link are passed to the app you pick in your phone's share menu. That app's own policy then applies.
  • Links you choose to open (for example a product page or a licence) open in your browser and are covered by that website's policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Label Lens is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob: banners, ads within lists, and occasional full-screen ads (for example when you return to the app or move between products). Ads never cover or delay a result. Google, as an independent provider, may collect and process the following data from your phone, including through its ad partners:

Your choices about ads

  • UK, EU, EEA and Switzerland: before ads load, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and whether information may be stored on your device. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, which use limited data (for example to prevent fraud and cap how often an ad is shown).
  • United States: where US state privacy laws apply (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut), Google's message lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. When that applies, it is available under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Canada and everywhere else: you can turn off ads personalisation and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads; wording varies by phone).

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · My Ad Center.

Legal bases (UK, EU, EEA and Switzerland)

Your profile, history and favourites are processed only on your phone, by you, to give you the app's features. Sending a barcode or a category to Open Food Facts is needed to give you the product information you asked for. For ads, personalised advertising and storing or reading information on your device rely on your consent, collected by Google's consent form; you can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. Google relies on its own legal bases for the processing it controls, as described in its privacy policy.

International transfers

Open Food Facts is based in the European Union. Google may process ad data in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy (such as standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app (unused cached products are removed after about 60 days).
  • We keep nothing, because we receive nothing.
  • Open Food Facts and Google keep data according to their own policies.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email collection or contact list access.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no crash-reporting service.
  • No location permission. (Google's ad systems may estimate an approximate location from your IP address, as described above.)
  • We don't sell your data. We have no data of yours to sell.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights such as access, correction, deletion, objecting to or restricting processing, data portability, withdrawing consent, and opting out of targeted advertising:

  • UK (UK GDPR), EU and EEA (GDPR) and Switzerland (Federal Act on Data Protection).
  • United States: state privacy laws such as those of California, Virginia, Colorado and Connecticut.
  • Canada: the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, in Quebec, Law 25.

Because your app data stays on your phone, you exercise most of these rights directly in the app or by deleting it. For data processed by Google for ads, use the choices above or contact Google. You can also contact us at saadghaffar97@gmail.com, and we will reply within one month.

You have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EU and EEA your national authority; in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner; in Canada the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information).

Children

Label Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app gives food information, and its ads are limited to content suitable for teens and adults.

Health information

The allergies and diet choices you enter stay on your phone and are used only to show the "for you" result on your device. Label Lens gives information from Open Food Facts, which may be incomplete. It is not medical advice. Always check the product packaging.

Languages

The app is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish. This policy is written in English; if you need help understanding it, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
